## Gates still open before a stable tag

Real departure/return presence transitions are complete for one production Life360-backed HA tracker and the selected Mobile App iPhone. [Current release readiness](CURRENT-RELEASE-READINESS.md) records both timelines and their different timestamp confidence. The iPhone had an eleven-hour HA state update gap and no independent GPS report time. These trips do not exercise the optional direct Life360 connection.

1. Establish genuine iPhone GPS report-time evidence and investigate its long daytime update gap; the production October 5 presence transitions passed but continuous reporting is unverified. The disposable server's lack of an external URL prevents its away-from-LAN test. A prior Friday trip exposed an HA Person repeatedly marked Home by an unselected stationary tracker while the iPhone was Away. Validate the new source-conflict explanation against that configuration; no historical card snapshots were retained for Friday. Do not expose household routes in public validation.
2. Exercise an unattended real provider outage and recovery. Confirm that unaffected trackers continue, that HomeCircle does not mislabel stale/unavailable data, and that a recovered source returns without losing selections. The paused-integration and fictional fallback rehearsals cover narrower cases.
3. Choose the supported HA Core version range and validate it. Tests and HACS metadata currently establish Core 2026.9.4 as the baseline, not every later release.
4. Build the exact stable candidate, rerun automated, HACS/hassfest, package, privacy/history/license, install/upgrade/restart/removal, browser, and physical-display checks. Then review the release notes and README in the tagged snapshot before publication.
5. Decide the stable public name and confirm that optional direct Life360, with its unsupported API and disclosed limitations, belongs in stable V0.1. Its adapter is implemented; direct-connection movement and provider failure acceptance are incomplete.
6. Complete the [licensing and service terms release checklist](#licensing-and-service-terms-release-checklist), recording the disposition of each finding for the exact stable candidate and its selected provider scope.

## Licensing and service terms release checklist

Added October 19, 2026 after reviewing beta 19 source and the published 109-file archive, SHA-256 `5f20d536e1b5378bef7f0b972bbfbc7ced911063aea16a0a8f56d2ffa1210cd2`. These are open release requirements. The technical review identified a missing inherited notice and unresolved licensing and service-permission questions; it did not determine legal enforceability or establish infringement. The inherited notice item is resolved in the local candidate; the other items remain open.

- [x] **Restore the inherited OpenTagViewer MIT notice and verify the packaged notice.** The local candidate restores the complete `Copyright (c) 2025 Shane B.` notice from the [pinned upstream license](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/parawanderer/OpenTagViewer/08a59cab551721afb9dc9f829ad31dae8d5bd400/LICENSE.TXT) in the existing packaged SDK license. A release regression verifies the derived `plist.py`, complete inherited notice and provenance through the archive/inventory path. See [combined isolation validation](STABLE-ISOLATION-VALIDATION.md). The reviewed published beta 19 archive remains unchanged and lacks this notice; this resolves the local candidate defect only.

- [ ] **Resolve the remaining inherited-code provenance.** Trace any retained protected expression through the [airy10 gist](https://gist.github.com/airy10/5205dc851fbd0715fcd7a5cdde25e7c8) and the [GrandSlam derivation](https://github.com/nythepegasus/grandslam). No explicit license was visible for the gist; GrandSlam's MIT notice contains placeholder copyright fields. Record pinned origins and the applicable permission or independent-implementation basis without inventing attribution. Replace or remove unsupported copied material where necessary. A reference to protocol facts alone does not establish copied protected expression, and this review did not establish infringement in either chain.

- [ ] **Complete the runtime dependency and distribution license inventory.** Record versions, origins, licenses, notices and distribution paths for authored code, vendored FindMy/emulator source, Leaflet, and direct and transitive runtime dependencies. Preserve the existing MIT and Leaflet BSD notices. Specifically assess [Unicorn's GPLv2 core](https://github.com/unicorn-engine/unicorn/blob/2.1.4/README.md) separately from its [BSD-labelled Python bindings](https://github.com/unicorn-engine/unicorn/blob/2.1.4/bindings/python/pyproject.toml). HA downloads the dependency separately and the emulator runs in a child process; neither fact alone resolves the combined-work/distribution question. MIT is not inherently incompatible with GPL. Record the applicable obligations before bundling wheels, native libraries or a prebuilt container; do not describe the complete runtime as exclusively MIT.

- [ ] **Resolve Apple software and service-use clearance for the chosen stable scope.** The [runtime provenance](../custom_components/homecircle/_findmy_runtime/PROVENANCE.md) documents downloading Apple's iTunes 12.8.3 payload, extracting CoreADI and executing its genuine binary under emulation. Keep Apple executables out of the release archive. Establish the exact applicable installer/library license and assess this extraction and emulated use, including applicable-law exceptions. The [published iTunes SLA](https://www.apple.com/legal/sla/docs/iTunes.pdf) restricts copying and reverse engineering in section 2.B and redistribution in section 3, but its identity with the pinned installer's accompanying agreement is unverified; section 2.A does not impose a blanket Apple-hardware-only restriction. Review [iCloud terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/icloud/en/terms.html), especially sections V.B.10, VI.A-B and VII.B: the automated-access wording sits within an interference/disruption provision, and VI.A-B are proprietary-use restrictions, not an explicit reverse-engineering clause. Record an appropriate permission/legal assessment and maintainer scope decision before stable publication. Make relevant provenance/license information available before runtime acquisition. Downloading separately, using the owner's account, or obtaining user consent does not itself establish Apple authorization.

- [ ] **Resolve Life360 service authorization and stable scope.** Review the [current prohibited uses](https://legal.corp.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/35494848369303-Life360-Terms-of-Service-2025-1), particularly III.B.10's restriction on access through means not provided or authorized by Life360. Cover native polling/login, place import and the optional Circle-wide family refresh using an external integration's authorization. Record the authorization or appropriate legal assessment and the resulting inclusion/exclusion decision; authorization was not established by this review. Low request volume, an unofficial client's open-source license and account-owner consent do not resolve that separate service restriction. Keep the provider-independent HA entity path available.

- [ ] **Verify map, geocoder and weather policy compliance on the stable build.** Check visible attribution, browser referrer and normal HTTP caching behavior against the [OSM tile policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/); do not add bulk/offline tile downloading against that service. Preserve Photon's identification, cache and request limits, and assess deployment scale against the demo server's [reasonable-use policy](https://github.com/komoot/photon#demo-server), which provides no availability guarantee. Verify the imagery/weather credits against [USGS National Map terms](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-are-terms-uselicensing-map-services-and-data-national-map) and [IEM terms](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/disclaimer.php). No obvious licensing blocker was identified for these services within the reviewed behavior; repeat checks if providers or request patterns change.

- [ ] **Review the public name, provider references and branding.** Complete appropriate name/trademark clearance for the stable product, review Apple/Life360 word marks and any logos or product imagery, and add accurate ownership/non-affiliation wording where appropriate. Apply [Apple's trademark guidance](https://www.apple.com/legal/intellectual-property/guidelinesfor3rdparties.html) without implying provider approval or endorsement. The review did not establish a trademark infringement.

- [ ] **Record the final release disposition and evidence.** Attach the exact source revision, release ZIP hash, dated dependency inventory with resolved runtime versions (including ranged requirements), notice/archive checks, relevant terms/license versions, assessments and maintainer decisions to the stable candidate's validation record. Identify any provider excluded or deferred from stable scope and update onboarding, README, privacy disclosures and release notes consistently. Disclaimers and experimental labels do not grant third-party permission; passing this checklist is not legal certification. No item is closed merely by adding this checklist.
