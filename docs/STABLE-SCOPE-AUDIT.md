## Stable gates, with historical field context

Real departure/return presence transitions are complete for one production Life360-backed HA tracker and the selected Mobile App iPhone. [Current release readiness](CURRENT-RELEASE-READINESS.md) records both timelines and their different timestamp confidence. The iPhone had an eleven-hour HA state update gap and no independent GPS report time. These trips do not exercise the optional direct Life360 connection.

1. Establish genuine iPhone GPS report-time evidence and investigate its long daytime update gap; the production October 5 presence transitions passed but continuous reporting is unverified. The disposable server's lack of an external URL prevents its away-from-LAN test. A prior Friday trip exposed an HA Person repeatedly marked Home by an unselected stationary tracker while the iPhone was Away. Validate the new source-conflict explanation against that configuration; no historical card snapshots were retained for Friday. Do not expose household routes in public validation.
2. Exercise an unattended real provider outage and recovery. Confirm that unaffected trackers continue, that HomeCircle does not mislabel stale/unavailable data, and that a recovered source returns without losing selections. The paused-integration and fictional fallback rehearsals cover narrower cases.
3. Choose the supported HA Core version range and validate it. Core 2026.9.4 remains the declared minimum. Exact beta 20 synthetic package checks cover native amd64 Core 2026.9.4/musl, actual HAOS 18.3 managed Core 2026.10.2/HACS and one Ubuntu 24.04/glibc Core 2026.10.2 target. Earlier frozen-parent results retain their own inputs; neither set establishes every later release or architecture. Choose and validate the final supported range.
4. Build the exact stable candidate, rerun automated, HACS/hassfest, package, privacy/history/license, install/upgrade/restart/removal, browser, and physical-display checks. Then review the release notes and README in the tagged snapshot before publication.
5. Decide the stable public name and confirm that optional direct Life360, with its unsupported API and disclosed limitations, belongs in stable V0.1. Its adapter is implemented; direct-connection movement and provider failure acceptance are incomplete.
6. Complete the [licensing and service terms release checklist](#licensing-and-service-terms-release-checklist), recording the disposition of each finding for the exact stable candidate and its selected provider scope.

## Licensing and service terms release checklist

Added October 19, 2026 after reviewing beta 19 source and the published 109-file archive, SHA-256 `5f20d536e1b5378bef7f0b972bbfbc7ced911063aea16a0a8f56d2ffa1210cd2`. The new GrandSlam omission, fetched-wheel notice gaps and remaining permission/distribution decisions are recorded below; technical inventory completion does not close those decisions.

A release regression verifies the derived `plist.py`, complete inherited notice and provenance through the archive/inventory path. See [combined isolation validation](https://github.com/jmhjr/HomeCircle/blob/d02a9bdb9027cb51c99165a09ba50d5b175f5fb9/docs/STABLE-ISOLATION-VALIDATION.md). The reviewed published beta 19 archive remains unchanged and lacks this notice. The October 20 audit independently compared the complete pinned notice with published beta 20.

- [ ] **Resolve the remaining inherited-code provenance and release the notice correction.** Trace any retained protected expression through the [airy10 gist](https://gist.github.com/airy10/5205dc851fbd0715fcd7a5cdde25e7c8) and the [GrandSlam derivation](https://github.com/nythepegasus/grandslam). The pinned comparisons now trace a 13-line client-format docstring and a nine-line CBC unpadding/comment block to GrandSlam. Its complete MIT notice was absent from beta 20 and contains literal unfilled copyright fields; the separate candidate retains it verbatim. No explicit license was visible for the gist. See the [vendored-origin inventory](BETA20-VENDORED-ORIGINS.md) for exact revisions and the narrower protocol-fact comparisons. Record pinned origins and the applicable permission or independent-implementation basis without inventing attribution. Replace or remove unsupported copied material where necessary. A reference to protocol facts alone does not establish copied protected expression, and this review did not establish infringement in either chain.

- [ ] **Resolve runtime dependency distribution obligations.** The [technical inventory](BETA20-DEPENDENCY-INVENTORY.md) now records the six direct requirements, observed 24-distribution Linux closure, exact public wheel evidence, vendored origins and bundled Leaflet notice. It is not a complete HA/OS or nested-component license audit. The inspected Unicorn wheel omits standalone license text for its included native libraries, and pyelftools omits its referenced Construct MIT notice. Candidate supplemental notices address retained text only; eventual installed-artifact, source and combined-work obligations remain open. Preserve the existing MIT and Leaflet BSD notices. Specifically assess [Unicorn's GPLv2 core](https://github.com/unicorn-engine/unicorn/blob/2.1.4/README.md) separately from its [BSD-labelled Python bindings](https://github.com/unicorn-engine/unicorn/blob/2.1.4/bindings/python/pyproject.toml). HA downloads the dependency separately and the emulator runs in a child process; neither fact alone resolves the combined-work/distribution question. MIT is not inherently incompatible with GPL. Record the applicable obligations before bundling wheels, native libraries or a prebuilt container; do not describe the complete runtime as exclusively MIT.

- [ ] **Resolve Apple software and service-use clearance for the chosen stable scope.** The [runtime provenance](../custom_components/homecircle/_findmy_runtime/PROVENANCE.md) documents downloading Apple's iTunes 12.8.3 payload, extracting CoreADI and executing its genuine binary under emulation. Keep Apple executables out of the release archive. Establish the exact applicable installer/library license and assess this extraction and emulated use, including applicable-law exceptions. The [published iTunes SLA](https://www.apple.com/legal/sla/docs/iTunes.pdf) restricts copying and reverse engineering in section 2.B and redistribution in section 3, but its identity with the pinned installer's accompanying agreement is unverified; section 2.A does not impose a blanket Apple-hardware-only restriction. Review [iCloud terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/icloud/en/terms.html), especially sections V.B.10, VI.A-B and VII.B: the automated-access wording sits within an interference/disruption provision, and VI.A-B are proprietary-use restrictions, not an explicit reverse-engineering clause. Record an appropriate permission/legal assessment and maintainer scope decision before stable publication. Make relevant provenance/license information available before runtime acquisition. Downloading separately, using the owner's account, or obtaining user consent does not itself establish Apple authorization.

- [ ] **Resolve Life360 service authorization and stable scope.** Review the [official newer terms](https://legal.corp.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/42963368361239-Life360-Terms-of-Service-2026-8), particularly I.B.10's restriction on access through means not provided or authorized by Life360. The official title is “2026/8” while its body says last updated April 14, 2026; those labels are retained without inferring a different effective date. The older 2025.1/III.B.10 pointer was the October 19 review reference. Determine the applicable agreement. Cover native polling/login, place import and the optional Circle-wide family refresh using an external integration's authorization. Record the authorization or appropriate legal assessment and the resulting inclusion/exclusion decision; authorization was not established by this review. Low request volume, an unofficial client's open-source license and account-owner consent do not resolve that separate service restriction. Keep the provider-independent HA entity path available.

- [ ] **Verify map, geocoder and weather policy compliance on the stable build.** Check visible attribution, browser referrer and normal HTTP caching behavior against the [OSM tile policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/); do not add bulk/offline tile downloading against that service.
