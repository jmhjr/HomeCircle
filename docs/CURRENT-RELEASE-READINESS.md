It samples every 15 seconds for at most 48 hours and saves changed projections plus a one-minute heartbeat. Records retain source state categories, active Person source as an anonymous slot, HA update/report times, normalized HomeCircle presence, map source label, focusability, driving evidence, and category counts. Addresses, portraits, raw responses, and credentials are omitted from output. The first coordinate-enabled sample passed live verification; no coordinate values are included in public documentation. Source labels may contain member names, so all capture files stay private. The output folder and credential file use private permissions. This tool removes only its own capture files older than seven days at the next run; stopping runs does not perform ongoing retention cleanup.

Samples are sequential, not atomic, and can miss transitions shorter than 15 seconds. Capture failures are recorded as gaps without exception bodies or credential-bearing URLs. A stopped process or sleeping/offline Mac cannot capture new states. Three focused synthetic checks passed for privacy projection, preservation of unknown report time, and missing-member behavior.
