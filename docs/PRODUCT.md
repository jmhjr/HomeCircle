# Product

HomeCircle is a provider-independent family location/presence platform for Home Assistant. Its audience is a household using an always-visible family dashboard, including portrait wall displays and optional DAKboard/TouchHub presentation.

The core turns configured HA location entities into a coherent household view. An optional built in Life360 connection can also create HA trackers using account credentials supplied in HomeCircle. No provider account is needed for the core. HomeCircle does not collect phone locations itself, guarantee continuous tracking, or replace the source services. Missing information must stay visibly unknown rather than be invented.

Member cards use the selected tracker's own location report time when it supplies one (for example, Life360 `last_seen`). Otherwise they show when HA last updated that selected tracker, labeled as an HA update rather than a verified GPS fix. The member details screen exposes both times and explains the distinction. An old update time alone does not change a stationary member to Unavailable. For HA Mobile App trackers only, members may opt in to a silent location request after 15 minutes without a tracker report; requests are capped at 50 per rolling 24 hours and separated by at least one minute. Sending a request does not guarantee a phone response.

## V0.1

1. Install an integration package through HACS and add HomeCircle using HA's config flow.
2. Select or create an HA Person for each new person or pet, then assign and configure that member's tracker. Show associated/active `device_tracker` sources where HA exposes them; allow explicit tracker selection when association cannot be determined safely. Do not guess relationships from entity names. Preserve existing tracker-only members and their conversion path.
3. Select a primary Home zone and additional places. Additional residences are assigned per member; ordinary places such as Work are not automatically Home.
4. Normalize family member identity, presence, optional location, source, timestamp confidence, and optional battery/status data.
5. Create a HomeCircle dashboard and card during first setup when none exists; also allow adding a card through the UI. The visual editor and resource registration require no manual YAML in a storage-mode dashboard.
6. Provide member cards, a basic map, usable overlapping markers, category summaries, and member/category/overview focus in a readable portrait layout.
7. Explain an HA Person source conflict when a linked tracker reports Home while the selected mobile tracker reports Away. Show the active source and a path to the HA Person settings without changing the Person or silently overriding HA's presence result. Multiple agreeing trackers remain valid.

A member at their assigned dorm/second home counts as Home. The default At Home focus includes only members at the primary family house. An explicit future or V0.1 option may include all residences, but must not change the default. Apply the same classification to member labels and counts. Unavailable is separate from Away. Driving requires available evidence; it is not inferred from being Away.

Pets, optional direct Life360 trackers, source labels, battery, report times, and evidence-based driving are implemented in beta 16. A new pet needs an HA Person and an existing pet tracker; the Person record does not create a GPS source. Battery and driving evidence remain optional, never prerequisites for basic presence. Existing tracker-only members remain readable and can be moved to a Person without losing their settings.

## Beyond V0.1

Additional built in tracker providers, address enrichment, drive time/ETA, weather/radar, recorded trip trails, more map-provider choices, and distance-apart summaries follow in separate milestones. Trip trails show available historical tracker reports with visible gaps rather than claiming an exact driven route. Beta 16 already includes pet onboarding and a kiosk view; their validated scope is recorded in the beta 16 reports.

Planned card polish replaces the visible Home, Away, Driving, and Unavailable words on person cards with accessible status icons. Planned location identification shows a configured place name or, when enabled and available, a street and city label consistently on each person's card and map label. Missing or stale location evidence must remain clear. See the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) for acceptance details and the pending lookup privacy decision.

The next candidate adds a long-press member details dialog over the dashboard, with a visible details control for keyboard and mouse users. It shows the current HA Person and selected tracker evidence, coordinates, update times, and troubleshooting guidance; administrators can continue to that person's settings and return to details with Back. Refresh reports what was checked. HA History opens as an overlay that closes back to HomeCircle when recorded samples are available, while a plotted trip trail remains a later enhancement. Future traffic-aware routes and radar overlays remain optional; the [provider shortlist](FUTURE-MAP-SERVICES.md) records current free allowances and limitations without committing the product to a service.

Optional alert setup can create user-approved HA automations for arrivals, departures, stale or unavailable trackers, and low battery. HomeCircle does not need a separate notification engine.

## Experience and success

A household with existing HA people should finish core setup without editing YAML or supplying provider credentials. A non-GPS tracker still yields presence, with an honest unavailable map position. OpenStreetMap street tiles are the default for new cards and send viewed map areas to the tile service; the map offers Street/Satellite buttons, zoom buttons that center visible pins while changing scale (and retain the current center when no pins are visible), and the editor offers a private background. Satellite uses USGS imagery, with detailed U.S. aerial coverage and limited detail elsewhere; images are historical, not live. The browser remembers the chosen external view for this card and dashboard. Private always disables external tiles and the view buttons. HomeCircle does not rewrite HA zones or remove existing cards.

Selecting a person card with a stale map-source report starts one bounded location check. HomeCircle-owned Life360 sources are rechecked in the cloud, with a shared one-minute selection cooldown and a persisted 50-attempt rolling 24-hour cap and existing provider backoff; this does not force a phone GPS fix. Home Assistant phone sources can receive a location-update request only when the member's existing automatic-request opt-in is enabled and the viewer has control permission for that exact tracker and its notification target. Phone attempts share the persisted limits with automatic requests: at least one minute apart and at most 50 per rolling 24 hours. Installed external Life360 trackers use `life360.update_location` when that supported action exists, with control permission for the exact tracker and the same persisted one-minute/50-per-day request limits. Other trackers report that requests are unsupported. Feedback distinguishes checking, requested, no newer report, cooldown and unavailable outcomes. Stale markers remain stale until the source supplies newer evidence; changing the active source alone never confirms a response. Overview/category selection does not trigger this action.

Person Details includes Recent activity: HomeCircle-observed status, map-source and report-freshness changes, plus automatic request attempts and completed selection-refresh outcomes. Retention is seven days with at most 100 events per configured member in local HA storage. Five latest events are shown first; older events expand separately from diagnostics. Observation time is distinct from tracker report time. Each startup/reload begins a new observation boundary; history is not backfilled from tracker records and gaps do not establish trips. No coordinates, routes, raw provider responses or account data are retained. Changing the saved configuration resets this history to prevent past inputs being exposed under changed access permissions. Removing HomeCircle removes its activity store.

Selecting a zero-count Away, Driving or Unavailable category preserves the current map center/zoom, hides pins and shows a category-specific empty message. The category stays selected and Everyone restores the household overview. A category with members but no usable positions is distinguished from a category with no members. Empty map selections no longer reset an existing view to the world map.

Outside a configured named place, person cards display the address supplied by the exact selected tracker providing the usable map position. Named places/residences take priority. Fresh tracker evidence displays At followed by the reported address; stale or unknown freshness displays Last reported instead. No address is borrowed from another tracker or from an unverified/withheld position. Away/Driving members with a usable position but no supplied address show Address unavailable. This uses an existing tracker address attribute, not an external geocoding lookup, and therefore depends on the selected integration supplying that field. The direct HomeCircle Life360 adapter currently does not supply it. Addresses are not added to Recent activity or field-capture retention.

The map bottom grip lets users adjust height with a mouse or touch. Height is remembered locally for this dashboard/card title; keyboard arrows adjust it and Home restores automatic sizing. Resizing preserves the current map view and selection.

Manual family refresh requests reports from all explicitly selected compatible Life360 Circle members, with its own persisted 50-attempt rolling 24-hour limit and one-minute cooldown. Individual/automatic request counters remain shared per tracker. Failed dispatches consume attempts; failed counter persistence prevents dispatch. Existing provider failure backoff remains independent of these local cooldowns.

The optional Radar toggle overlays Iowa Environmental Mesonet U.S. NEXRAD radar above Street or Satellite and below person markers. Radar starts off, offers opacity adjustment and a reflectivity color guide, and retains person/Everyone zoom and map resizing behavior. It refreshes tile requests about every five minutes while visible. The timestamp describes the observed mosaic or forecast frame rather than each individual radar scan; missing metadata is labeled time unavailable; close zoom remains coarse and blank tiles do not prove clear weather. Radar failures do not change household presence or remove the base map. Private map mode prevents radar requests.

Radar opacity and color guide are hidden by default. HomeCircle settings offers “Show radar opacity and color guide”, saved for this browser and shown only when Radar is enabled. The display setting does not change tracker configuration or issue location requests.

Default radar opacity is 40%; optional controls initialize to the same value.

Radar playback offers Past 30 min (observed mosaics in 5-minute steps) and Future 2 hr (HRRR predictions in 15-minute steps). Each frame shows its date, local clock time, timezone and Observed/Forecast label. Observed mosaics and forecasts use explicit timestamps; forecast playback uses one model run and displays its initialization time. Metadata failures or expired forecasts do not fabricate times. Playback is opt-in, waits for loaded tiles, stops on map movement or hidden/disconnected views, and disposes historical layers before clearing their listeners. Opacity remains 40% and its controls remain optional.

Map updates preserve a manually chosen view; the recenter icon restores the current selection. Temporary connection failures display last received locations with a warning. Automatic phone location requests wait at least 15 minutes between attempts and pause after three unanswered automatic attempts until the HA tracker reports again; manual requests keep their one-minute cooldown.
