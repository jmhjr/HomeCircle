Tracker-supplied report advance is distinct from an HA last-reported/updated receipt; the latter is labeled GPS-unconfirmed. Assigned sources can be observed even when Person or another tracker supplies the map. Multi-source member feedback lists provider outcomes. Source changes and late evidence do not rewrite frozen results. No raw credentials, cloud IDs or remote reply bodies enter feedback.
